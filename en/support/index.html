Tutor Scheduler & Lesson Packs is a local-first lesson and student manager for independent teachers, tutors and coaches. If you run into a problem, email a written description. Screenshots or a screen recording can help when appropriate.

Contact support: fivechaoice@gmail.com

What to include in a support request

  1. Your Tutor Scheduler & Lesson Packs version and build, available under Settings & Studio → About Tutor Scheduler & Lesson Packs → Version.
  2. What you were doing before the issue appeared, such as scheduling, completing a lesson, correcting a record, renewing a lesson pack, sharing a lesson update, backing up or restoring.
  3. Any message shown on screen, plus a screenshot or screen recording if useful.
  4. Whether the issue is still present after reopening the app.

To protect privacy, please do not send full bank account details, unnecessary student-sensitive information, or an entire JSON backup unless support first explains why a specific minimum amount of information is needed.

Frequently asked questions

Does opening the Share Sheet mean my lesson update was sent?

No. Tutor Scheduler & Lesson Packs prepares the lesson-update text and opens the system sharing flow when you choose to share. You decide the destination and whether to send it. Opening a sharing destination is never treated as proof that a message was sent.

What if I deducted the wrong lesson?

Use the correction or undo options available for that lesson state. Tutor Scheduler & Lesson Packs updates the related lesson-pack balance and history according to the app's current correction rules.

What is included in a JSON backup?

A backup may contain teacher or studio settings, students, lesson packs and lesson records. Treat the backup as private teaching-business data and store it only in a location you trust.

How do I restore my data?

Under Settings & Studio, choose the JSON backup restore option and select a valid backup with Apple's Files interface. Tutor Scheduler & Lesson Packs checks the backup and shows a preview before replacing current local data after your confirmation.

Does Tutor Scheduler & Lesson Packs upload student records to its own server?

No. The current version has no Tutor Scheduler & Lesson Packs account system or developer-operated cloud service for your lesson records. Core app data stays on your device. Data leaves the app only when you choose an external action such as system sharing or exporting a backup.

Do I need an account or subscription?

No. The current version does not require a Tutor Scheduler & Lesson Packs account and does not include an in-app subscription.
