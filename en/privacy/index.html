LessonClear Privacy Policy

LessonClear (formerly Tutor Scheduler & Lesson Packs) | Version 1.4 | Last updated November 23, 2026

LessonClear is a local-first schedule and prepaid lesson-pack ledger for independent tutors, music and language teachers, and one-to-one coaches. It requires no account and uses no developer server, cloud sync, third-party analytics, ads, or tracking.

1. Information you may enter

Depending on how you use LessonClear, you may enter teacher or studio details, student names and contact notes, subjects and levels, lesson packs, schedules, lesson notes, homework, lesson fees, and correction records.

If you record information about students, parents, or minors, keep only what is genuinely needed for teaching and lesson administration, and obtain any consent or other lawful basis required where you operate.

Optional remittance or bank details are stored only on your device and may be included in a JSON backup you export; the developer does not collect them.

2. Where data is stored and device backups

The current version does not require a LessonClear account, does not include built-in iCloud or cloud sync, and does not use a developer-operated server to receive the lesson-management data described above. Core data is stored locally in the app's storage on your Apple device.

The local database is not excluded from iOS device backups, so it will be backed up along with your own iPhone or iCloud device backup depending on your own device backup settings.

3. Whether we collect your lesson-management data

The current version does not include advertising SDKs, third-party behavioral analytics SDKs, or tracking SDKs, and it does not use a developer-operated network API to receive your student or lesson-management data. As a result, the current version does not collect that lesson-management data on the app developer's or advertising/analytics partners' servers.

4. System Sharing

Sharing uses the iOS system share sheet, which may include LINE or any other app. LessonClear has no special LINE integration. You choose the destination and decide whether to send. The app never sends anything by itself, and opening a destination is not proof of sending. The chosen service's own terms and privacy policy apply.

5. Clipboard

LessonClear writes to the system clipboard only when you tap Copy or use a visible fallback option. It never reads your clipboard secretly in the background.

6. JSON backup and restore

You can export a JSON backup and restore from a valid backup selected with Apple's Files interface. A backup may contain teacher, student, lesson-pack, and lesson information, so choose a storage location and sharing destination you trust.

Backups you save to Files, iCloud Drive, or another third-party location are retained and deleted according to your choices and that service's rules. When restoring, LessonClear validates the backup and shows a preview before replacing current local data after your confirmation.

7. In-App Purchase and Restore

LessonClear offers a one-time, non-consumable Lifetime in-app purchase. It is not a subscription and does not auto-renew. The App Store shows the price in your local currency before you buy. Apple handles all payments and refunds, and LessonClear never sees your card numbers. If a purchase is refunded, the Lifetime entitlement is disabled.

If you reinstall the app or switch to another device using the same Apple Account, you can tap Restore Purchases on the purchase screen or in Settings to restore your Lifetime entitlement.

8. Retention and deletion

Local data remains in the app's storage until you change it, replace it through a confirmed restore, erase the device or app data, or remove the app. External backups you exported and your own iPhone or iCloud device backups are not automatically deleted when app data is removed; manage those copies in Files, your device backup settings, cloud storage, or the relevant third-party service.

The current version has no LessonClear server-side user account, so there is no separate LessonClear server account dataset that you need to ask us to delete.

9. Tracking, advertising, and analytics

  • The current version does not display third-party advertising.
  • It does not integrate advertising-tracking SDKs.
  • It does not integrate third-party behavioral analytics SDKs.
  • It does not use your data to create a cross-app or cross-site advertising profile.

10. Information about minors

LessonClear is designed for teachers managing their own teaching practice; it is not a child-directed account, social, or advertising service. Teachers may still enter necessary information about minor students as part of lesson administration. Avoid recording sensitive information that is unrelated to teaching or lesson management.

11. Security

LessonClear uses a local-first design, minimizes data transmission, and uses controlled backup/restore and data-consistency checks to reduce risk. No storage method can guarantee absolute security. Protect your device with an appropriate screen lock and keep exported backups secure.

12. Policy updates

The current version now includes a one-time Apple in-app purchase ("Lifetime"), while still having no developer-operated paid backend, user accounts, cloud sync, or third-party analytics. If a future version adds accounts, cloud sync, third-party analytics, a developer-operated paid backend, or another feature that changes data handling, we will update this policy and the relevant App Store privacy disclosures to match the actual version.

13. Contact
